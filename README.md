# Super-Store-Sales-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to track Super Store sales performance, profitability, customer patterns, and provide a 15-day sales forecast.

Key Metrics -
Sales: $1.6M, Profit: $175.26K, Quantity: 22K, Avg Shipping: 4 Days
Top Payment Mode: COD(43%)
Top Segment: Consumer(48%)
Top Region: West(33%) followed by East(29%)
Top Category: Office Supplies($0.64M)

Key Insights -
1. Peak Season: Strong sales and profit spikes during Q4 (Oct–Dec).
2. Customer Behavior: High reliance on Cash on Delivery (43%) and Standard Shipping.
3. Regional Dominance: West and East regions drive over 60% of total sales.

Final Conclusion -
1. Leverage the 15-day forecast to optimize inventory before demand surges.
2. Streamline logistics to reduce the average 4-day shipping cycle.
3. Focus marketing efforts on high-performing product categories (Phones, Chairs) and leading states.

Tools Used -
Power BI (Data Modeling, DAX, Time-Series Forecasting)
Power Query (ETL & Data Cleaning)
